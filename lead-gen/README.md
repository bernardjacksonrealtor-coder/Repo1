# Bernard Jackson Lead Engine

Buyer and seller leads from Google search, AI answers (AEO/GEO), and social, all landing in
Lofty, tracked in one sheet, reviewed every Friday.

Website: bernardsellsflhomes.com (Lofty CMS) · CRM: Lofty · Automation: Zapier · Extra hosting: Netlify

## Straight talk before you start

- **Google SEO and AI-answer traffic take 3–6 months.** Do it now so it pays off by spring, but
  your leads for the next 90 days come from Google Business Profile, Facebook, and follow-up speed.
- **Every lead must land in Lofty with a source.** If you can't tell where a lead came from, you
  can't adjust. That's what `04-tracking.md` fixes. Do it first.
- **Follow-up beats lead volume.** A lead answered in 5 minutes is worth ten answered tomorrow.
  Build the Smart Plans before you spend money on ads.

## 90-day plan

**Weeks 1–2: Foundation (nothing else works without this)**
1. Set up tracking: Lofty lead sources, UTM rules, Zapier → Google Sheet (`04-tracking.md`).
2. Google Business Profile fully filled out, 10 photos, first 3 posts (`01`).
3. Lofty SEO settings: site title, meta descriptions, Featured Areas for all 4 cities (`01`).
4. Paste the schema code into Lofty (`02`).
5. Confirm Smart Plans are on for New Web Buyer and Seller/Home Value leads.
6. Ask 10 past clients/friends for a Google review (script in `01`).

**Weeks 3–6: Content engine**
1. Publish 2 question-pages a week from the list in `02` (start with the seller ones — sellers are
   your highest-value leads).
2. One GBP post a week, linked to the matching page.
3. Social running daily (`03`), every link UTM-tagged.
4. Run the AI-mention test once (baseline).

**Weeks 7–12: Measure and double down**
1. Every Friday: fill the scoreboard, cut what's not producing, double what is.
2. Week 8: boost the 2 best-performing organic FB posts ($30–50 each), to a Lofty landing page.
3. Week 10: if GBP has 15+ reviews, price out Google Local Services Ads.
4. Week 12: rerun the AI-mention test, compare to baseline.
